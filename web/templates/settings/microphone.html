Wake acknowledgements

When PETEY hears his wake name followed by a pause, he plays a preloaded voice clip or an immediate local chime and listens for the request. Choose spoken “Yeah?” only if you prefer it; browser speech can start slowly. Continuing directly into a command skips the acknowledgement. Add WAV, MP3, OGG, OPUS, M4A, AAC, FLAC, or WEBM files to the voice folder. Put interruption responses in its interrupts/ subfolder; PETEY chooses randomly and falls back to voice-cues/default/interrupts/.
